Error 404

A page is missing. Nothing else is.

You asked for an address that is not here. Press back, or start again from the top, and this mistake costs you the few seconds it took to read this line.

That is what a safe failure looks like. It is cheap, it is obvious the moment it happens, and it leaves you free to try again.

Every technology we have made safe, we made safe that way. Seatbelts came after crashes. Aviation built an entire discipline out of investigating wreckage. Each one worked because the failure was survivable, local, and slow enough to study.

A broken link is the good kind of mistake. Not every mistake gets to be that kind.

错误 404

少了一个页面。别的什么都没少。

你要的地址不在这里。按下返回，或者从头开始，这个错误的代价，不过是你读完这行字的几秒钟。

安全的失败就是这个样子：代价很低，发生的那一刻就看得见，而且留下了再试一次的余地。

我们把每一项技术变安全，走的都是这条路。安全带出现在车祸之后。航空业把调查残骸做成了一整门学科。它们之所以奏效，是因为那些失败可以幸存、影响局部，而且慢到来得及研究。

链接失效是那种好的错误。并不是每个错误，都有机会属于这一类。
